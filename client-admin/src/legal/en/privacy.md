# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective: [date]

## In short

- We keep data only to run the school's work and keep the service safe.
- One school can never see another school's data.
- We never sell data or use it for ads.

1. **What we keep.** What the school gives us: student name, date of birth, photo, class, attendance, marks, fees; guardian and staff name, phone, email. And, for security, the time, IP address and browser of each sign-in.
2. **Why.** Only to run the school's work — attendance, results, fees, messages to guardians — and to keep the service safe.
3. **Who can see it.** Only people your school allows. One school can never see another school's data. Our staff enter only with your permission, to help you. When our staff sign in as a person of your school, that entry is recorded and marked, and the school Admin can end it.
4. **Who helps us.** SMS companies, WhatsApp (Meta) and email services to send messages; Cloudflare for security and backups; Google or Facebook if you sign in with them; Sentry to find errors. Each gets only what it needs for that job.
5. **Where it is kept.** [country / provider]. Backups are encrypted, in Cloudflare R2.
6. **How long.** As long as the school uses the service. After it leaves and asks us in writing, data is deleted; older backup copies drop away as our backups are rotated.
7. **Security.** Encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted backups, passwords never stored as plain text, accounts lock after repeated wrong sign-ins.
8. **Children's data.** Students are children. The school keeps their data and photos with guardian consent. If a guardian refuses photo consent, the school's photo upload is turned off and the photo is no longer shown. Copies already in backups or print records may remain until they are rotated out.
9. **Your rights.** See your data, correct it, ask for it to be deleted. Guardians and students ask their school first; the school tells us.
10. **If something goes wrong.** If data leaks, or we suspect it did, we tell the school within [72 hours].
11. **Cookies.** Only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. No advertising cookies.
12. **Contact.** [email]

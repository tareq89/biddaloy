# Data Agreement
Version 1.0 · Effective: [date]

This agreement is between the school and [company name]. It says how we handle the school's data.

1. **The school owns its data.** All student, guardian and staff data belongs to the school. We process it only on the school's instructions, to provide SchoolManagerP.
2. **Staff confidentiality.** Our staff who can reach school data are bound to keep it confidential. They enter a school's account only to help, with the school's permission.
3. **Security measures.** Encrypted connections (HTTPS); encrypted backups every hour; passwords never stored as plain text; accounts lock after repeated wrong sign-ins.
4. **Breach notice.** If school data leaks, or we suspect it did, we tell the school within [72 hours].
5. **Who helps us (sub-processors).** SMS companies (e.g. GreenWeb, MiM SMS), WhatsApp (Meta) and email services to send messages; Cloudflare for security and backups; Google or Facebook if you sign in with them; Sentry to find errors. Each gets only what it needs for that job.
6. **When you leave.** Before leaving, the school can download all its data to Excel. After it leaves, on written request, we permanently delete the school's data.

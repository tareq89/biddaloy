# Reports glossary (Epic 8.15, D21)

Every word a user reads on a report screen comes from this list. Bangla first, English second.
If a word is not here, ask before inventing one. The words that failed in testing are added at the bottom with what replaced them.

## What we say

| ------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |

## Rules

- Money is always **বিল / জমা / বাকি**. Never debit, credit or ledger.
- Plain error text sits next to the control that caused it. An empty result says what to change ("Try another class").
- Numbers and dates use the shared formatters (Bangla digits when the school's language is Bangla).
- A cell hidden for privacy shows "—" with a tooltip, never "0".

# Printing ID cards

**Who can do this:** Admin and Accountant. (Staff cards also need permission to
see staff HR records.)

You can start printing from wherever you already are. It takes at most three
actions.

## Print one card

1. Open the student (or staff member).
2. Press **Print ID card**, or open the **Documents** tab and press it there.
3. In the preview, check the card and press **Print**.

The **Documents** tab also shows the person's photo and everything printed for
them before. Add or replace a student's photo there.

## Print a selection

1. Open **Students** (or **Staff**).
2. Tick the people you want.
3. Press **Print ID cards (n)** at the top of the list.

## Print a whole class

1. Open **Students**.
2. Choose the **class** and the **section** in the filters.
3. Press **Print ID cards for whole Class 6-A**.

You can also press **Ctrl+K** and type "Print student ID card" to pick people or a
whole section by name.

## In the preview

- Cards are shown as they will print, with real names and photos.
- Choose your **printer** ([why](printers-and-calibration.md#why-do-i-choose-the-printer-myself)).
- A warning panel lists cards that need attention: text that does not fit, a
  missing photo, an empty field. Fix them, or choose **Print anyway**.
- If the class is big, the cards are split into **batches**. Print one batch,
  confirm it, and the next batch unlocks.

## "Did all print correctly?"

After each batch, SchoolManager asks: **Did all 40 cards print correctly?**

- **Yes, all printed** — done. The batch is recorded.
- **Some failed…** — tick the cards that did not print well (a jam, a smudge)
  and confirm. Then press **Reprint n failed** to print just those cards.

Please always answer. The record is only complete when you do.

## Reprinting a card

A card gets worn or lost. Open the person's **Documents** tab, or **Reports →
Printables & documents**, find the card and press **Reprint**. The new card has
the next **copy number**. If your design has a copy label (for example "Copy 2"),
it prints on the card so nobody mistakes it for the first one.

## The history

**Reports → Printables & documents** lists every card printed: who, when, which
design version, which printer, and whether it printed correctly. Use the filters
to find a card. Open a row to see exactly what was printed.

## Cancel a lost card (revoke)

**Who can do this:** Admin.

1. Open the card in the history and press **Revoke**.
2. Write a reason (for example "Lost") and press **Revoke card**.

A revoked card cannot be reprinted. Anyone who scans its QR code will see it is
**no longer valid**.

## What the QR code shows

Each card has a QR code. Scanning it opens a small public page. It shows only:

- the type of document (Student ID card),
- the holder's name,
- the school,
- the date it was issued,
- the copy number,
- and **VALID** or **REVOKED**.

It never shows a photo, a phone number or an ID number. No login is needed, so a
shopkeeper or guard can check a card with any phone.

## If something goes wrong

- **The print window did not open.** Allow pop-ups for SchoolManager.
- **"No printer yet".** Add one in **Settings → Printers**.
- **"No template yet".** Create and publish one in **Print templates**.
- **A photo is missing.** Add it on the student's **Documents** tab.

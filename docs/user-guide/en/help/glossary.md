## The three states: where your work has reached <a href="#states" id="states"></a>

On every page where something is saved, these three names always mean the same thing.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Changed on screen only** | The mark is beside the name, but you have not submitted yet. Nothing has reached the system. |
| **Saved on this device** | The work is kept on your phone or computer. It goes to the school's system when the internet is back. |
| **Reached the school's system** | The work is submitted to the school's system. You or anyone else will see it on any device. |

## Fees and money <a href="#money" id="money"></a>

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="chalan"></a>[**Invoice**](../finance/invoices.md) | The paper made after money is received. It shows which bills the money went to. You do not make it yourself; recording a payment makes it. | Printing the invoice after a payment and giving it to the guardian. |
| <a id="credit-note"></a>[**Credit note**](../finance/invoices.md#confirm) | The paper made against the original invoice when a payment is reversed. It shows the reason for the reversal. | The credit note for reversing a payment taken in the wrong student's name. |
| <a id="reversal"></a>[**Reverse payment**](../finance/payments.md#task-reverse) | Undoing a wrong payment in full. The original payment is not deleted; a new reversal entry is added. A part of a payment cannot be reversed. | Reversing a payment written as 5,000 instead of 500, then recording it again. |
| <a id="reversal-entry"></a>[**Reversal entry**](../finance/payments.md#confirm) | The new row made when a payment is reversed. It stays linked to the original payment. It cannot be reversed itself. | **Reversal entry** in the **Status** column of the list. |
| <a id="extra-approval"></a>[**Extra approval**](../finance/payments.md#task-reverse) | Asking for an admin's password or code before a risky action. If there is another admin, you cannot approve your own action. | The code of another admin before a payment is reversed. |
| <a id="wallet"></a>[**Credit balance**](../finance/record-payment.md#task-change) | Extra money kept in a student's name. It is used on the next bill. | Receiving 1,000 taka, paying an 800 taka bill, keeping 200 as credit balance. |
| <a id="discount"></a>[**Discount**](../finance/record-payment.md#task-discount) | The part taken off a bill. The payment shows who gave the discount. | A 100 taka discount for siblings. |
| <a id="waiver"></a>[**Waiver**](../finance/fines.md#task-waive) | Forgiving money in full or in part. | Waiving the fine for an absence caused by illness. |
| <a id="fee-batch"></a>[**Billing round**](../finance/generate-fees.md) | A group of fee bills made in one go. A round holds the period, the fee, how many students and the total amount. | The January tuition round, 420 students. |
| <a id="recurring"></a>[**Recurring bills**](../finance/recurring-schedules.md) | A rule that makes fee bills by itself on a set day each month or week. | The monthly tuition bill on the 1st of every month. |
| <a id="overdue"></a>[**Overdue**](../finance/dues.md) | The bill's due date has passed and the money is not paid. | A bill due on 10 January still unpaid on 15 January. |
| <a id="share-link"></a>[**Share link**](../finance/invoices.md#task-share) | A link to see an invoice that opens without signing in. It stops working after 30 days. Making a new link turns the old one off. | Sending the invoice link to a guardian on WhatsApp. |

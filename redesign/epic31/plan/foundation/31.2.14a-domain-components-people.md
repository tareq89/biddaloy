# [31.2.14a] ui — people & account components to kit look: StudentPicker, SessionList, profile / guardian contact / password / push cards

## Goal
The shared components the portal account, `/security` and the guardian-portal pages place (they may not
edit them, CONTRACT Addendum 8) look like their page mockups: kit chips in `StudentPicker`; one framed,
divided list in `SessionList` with device icon, IP, sign-in time, total and a `ConfirmDialog` for
"sign out everywhere"; the four account cards with `text-h2` titles, a two-column field grid and a
right-aligned Save footer. No prop is removed; every new prop is optional.
